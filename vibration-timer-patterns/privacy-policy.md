# Privacy Policy — Vibration Timer & Patterns

Effective date: 3 October 2026  
Publisher: Techtenstein Services Private Limited  
Android package: com.vibration.sessiontimer  
Contact: [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com)

Vibration Timer & Patterns runs timed vibration patterns on your device. It does not require an account or access to your photos, documents, contacts, microphone, or precise location. It is a comfort utility, not a medical treatment.

## Information and purposes

| Information | How it is used | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your selected pattern, timer and intensity | Restores your choices the next time you open the app | Stored in the app's private storage on your device; not sent to a Techtenstein server |
| Advertising and consent information, when ads are enabled | Requests consent where required; delivers, measures and secures ads | Google Mobile Ads, Google's User Messaging Platform, and the Unity Ads mediation adapter may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostics. Their handling is governed by their own policies |
| A support email you choose to send | Responds to your support request | The message and your email address are handled through Techtenstein's support mailbox; the app has no in-app email collection form |

The app does not upload your vibration settings or session content to a Techtenstein server. The current release does not offer in-app purchases or display a Remove Ads purchase interface. It does not collect card details. Ads are not requested during an active vibration session.

The advertising providers' practices are described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Google's partner-sites notice](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and [Unity's game-player privacy information](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-faq). Techtenstein does not sell your app settings or support messages.

## Your choices and retention

You can change your pattern, timer and intensity in the app. You can delete locally saved settings by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. If regional advertising privacy controls apply, the app offers a Privacy choices control so you can review or change consent. You can request access to or deletion of a support message by emailing [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com). Techtenstein deletes support emails within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is legally required.

Local settings remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall. Advertising data is retained by Google and, when used for mediation, Unity under their own policies; Techtenstein cannot delete those providers' records from within this app. Use the relevant advertising provider's privacy controls for those records.

## Security, children and changes

Local choices are held in Android app-private storage. The app uses provider SDKs for consent and ads. The app is intended for adults aged 18 and older; it does not include an in-app age gate or age verification.

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. For a material change in app data practices, Techtenstein will also notify users in the app before or with the relevant app update. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com).
