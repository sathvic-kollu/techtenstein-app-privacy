# Privacy Policy — Control Center

Effective date: October 29, 2026  
Publisher: Techtenstein Apps  
Android package: app.controlcenter.quicksettings.ios  
Contact: [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com)

## Information handled on your device

Control Center does not require an account. App preferences, panel settings, and selected shortcuts are stored on your device in the app's private storage.

The app offers optional device controls and screen capture features. To show its floating control panel, it uses Android's “display over other apps” access after you enable it. Optional accessibility access performs the system action you select; the service is configured not to retrieve window content. Optional notification-listener access supports media controls. Android may make notification information available to services you enable; Control Center uses this access for media controls and does not save or send notification contents to the publisher. The app also uses Android system access for the device controls you choose to use, such as changing sound, display, connectivity, or rotation settings.

When you start a screenshot or screen recording, Android presents its system consent prompt. The app saves screenshots and recordings on your device. Microphone audio is included only if you choose that recording option and grant microphone permission. The app does not send those captures or recordings to the publisher. If you share a file using Android's share controls, you choose the recipient and any receiving service.

Camera access is used for the flashlight control. The app does not use that access to take photos or record video.

## Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may collect and share information such as IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The information collected and whether ads are personalized depend on Google's services, the app's configuration, your region, and your consent choices.

UMP presents consent messages when required and provides privacy choices when available in your region. You can also use Android's settings to reset or limit the advertising ID. For more information, see Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure), and [UMP privacy guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy).

## Information sent to the publisher

Control Center does not require an app account. App preferences, screen captures, and recordings remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them. Google Mobile Ads and UMP process information as described above.

## Purchases and verification

When you buy or restore the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase, the app sends the store-issued purchase token or purchase ID, store name, app package, and product ID over HTTPS to our ControlCenter purchase-verification service hosted on Cloudflare. The service checks the purchase with Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store to confirm the entitlement, acknowledge eligible purchases, and handle refunds or revocations. Payment details are handled by the store; our verifier does not receive your card information. A purchase identifier is a sensitive credential and should not be shared publicly.

Our verifier does not save raw purchase tokens or purchase IDs. It keeps a SHA-256 hash of the purchase credential, the store, app and product identifiers, entitlement status, and update time. Cloudflare processes network information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the service. Our application code uses a secret-keyed hash of the address for rate limiting; it does not save the raw address or enable application request logs. Cloudflare may process service information under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Purchase information is used for app functionality, entitlement verification, and fraud prevention, not advertising.

## Retention and deletion

App preferences are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Screenshots and recordings saved in device media storage may remain after uninstall; delete those files separately using your gallery or file manager. Google handles advertising information under its own retention and deletion practices.

Hashed purchase records are retained while the verification service operates to support restoration and prevent revoked purchases from being reactivated; they do not currently have an automatic expiry. Rate-limit hashes normally remain for less than about two minutes and are cleaned on subsequent verification requests; if traffic stops, they remain until the next request. A verified entitlement may be cached locally for up to 24 hours (normally six hours), after which another store verification is required. Contact us to request deletion of information handled by the publisher. We may need proof of the relevant purchase to locate its record; do not send passwords, payment-card details, or raw purchase tokens by email. Removing purchase-verification records may affect purchase restoration and does not cancel or refund a store transaction.

## Your choices

You can decline optional Android permissions in system settings. Features that need a declined permission will be unavailable. You can disable the floating panel, accessibility access, or notification access from Android settings. You can review available advertising privacy choices from the app's privacy choices entry when it is shown.

## Intended audience

Control Center is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Advertising remains subject to applicable consent choices and store policies.
