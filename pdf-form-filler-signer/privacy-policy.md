# Privacy Policy — PDF Form Filler & Signer

Effective date: September 27, 2026  
Publisher: Techtenstein Services Private Limited  
Android package: com.pdf.formfiller.signer  
Contact: [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com)

## App content and permissions

You choose documents through Android's file picker. The app imports a private copy for editing and can add text, signature drawings, images and stamps. Optional camera access lets you capture an image for a document. Documents and signatures are processed locally; the app does not upload them to a Techtenstein server. Exporting or sharing deliberately sends a copy to the destination or app you select, which handles it under its own policy.

The app does not require a Techtenstein account. Permissions can be declined; features that need the declined permission will be unavailable.

## Advertising and consent

The app contains banner, native, interstitial and app-open advertising. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform handle advertising and regional consent. Advertising services can collect and share IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device and advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [partner-services information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Consent availability and ad personalization depend on your region and choices. Where required, use the app's Privacy choices control to review consent; Android also provides advertising-ID controls.



## Retention and your choices

Imported documents, working files and saved app data may remain in private storage until removed, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Exported and shared copies remain at their chosen destinations; clearing this app does not delete them. Revoke camera access in Android Settings.

Support emails are optional. Your email address and message are used to respond through our support mailbox. We delete support emails within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a longer period is legally required. Email contact@techtenstein.com to request access or deletion. Do not send sensitive documents unless necessary for your request.

Advertising and service providers retain their records under their own policies. Contact the relevant provider for records it controls. Techtenstein does not sell your app content or support messages.

## Security and updates

App-private data uses Android's application storage protections. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Contact [contact@techtenstein.com](mailto:contact@techtenstein.com) with privacy questions.
